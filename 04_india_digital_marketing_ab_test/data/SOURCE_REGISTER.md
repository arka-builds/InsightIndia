# Data provenance — marketing A/B experiment

`india_marketing_ab_test_synthetic.csv` is a generated randomized example with 20,000 synthetic users. State, city tier, device, channel, conversion and spend fields are simulated. It is not a real Indian campaign, platform export or observed customer experiment.

Use this dataset to demonstrate experimental design, conversion-rate calculations, confidence intervals and a two-proportion test only. A real business decision requires first-party randomized campaign logs, a pre-defined primary metric, sample-size/power planning, guardrail metrics, assignment validation and a pre-registered analysis plan.
