# Data provenance — MoRTH road safety

## Primary source
Government of India, Ministry of Road Transport and Highways, *Road Accidents in India 2023* (published 2025), Section 5:
- Table 5.1, state/UT-wise road accidents, 2019–2023 (printed page 66).
- Table 5.6, state/UT-wise persons killed, 2019–2023 (printed page 71).

Official PDF: https://morth.gov.in/backend/documents/uploaded/Road-Accident-in-India-2023-Publications.pdf

The CSV is a manual transcription of the published tables into long format. It contains 37 state/UT labels, 5 years, and two measures. Values marked not_reported are blank in the source; they are not converted to zero. DNH and Daman & Diu presentation follows the source table footnote. Annual sums reconcile to the all-India totals printed by MoRTH for both measures and all five years.

## Limitations
- The data is an administrative report of reported road accidents and fatalities, not a measure of all incidents or exposure-adjusted risk.
- Absolute counts should not be interpreted as a per-capita or per-vehicle risk ranking.
- The source publication covers 2023; do not describe it as real-time or 2026 data.
- No state-wise injury series is included in this project extract.
